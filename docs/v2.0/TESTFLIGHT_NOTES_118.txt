A little tribute to Leonidas.

When the Great Dane wins, tap his portrait: he celebrates with “Good boy, Leo!” Tap him three times to reveal “Goodnight, handsome.” This works in solo battles, winning teams and tournaments.

Please try repeated taps, a rematch, and Reduce Motion. The tribute should never change the result or award extra coins. Build 117’s easier-to-read stories and supporting text are retained.
