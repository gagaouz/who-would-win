## Description

Who would win: a gorilla or a grizzly? A Great Dane or a wolf? A T-Rex or a Triceratops?

Animal vs Animal lets kids pick any two creatures and find out. The winner isn't random: it's decided by real size, speed and special abilities, so kids learn how the animal world actually works. Every battle comes with an action-packed story, a "WHY?" card explaining what decided it, and a fun fact.

PICK FROM 143 CREATURES
- Real animals from land, sea and sky: lions, tigers, great white sharks, orcas, gorillas, crocodiles, eagles and more
- Pets and farm animals: dogs, cats, hamsters, goldfish, horses, cows, geese and more
- Bugs and tiny creatures: army ant, bullet ant, praying mantis, scorpion
- Prehistoric Pack: T-Rex, Triceratops, Megalodon, Saber-Tooth Tiger and more
- Fantasy Pack: Dragon, Kraken, Unicorn, Griffin, Phoenix, Hydra and more
- Mythic Beasts Pack: Thunderbird, Sphinx, Chimera, Kirin and more
- Olympus Pack: Zeus, Poseidon, Athena, Hercules and the Greek gods
- Make your own: type in a creature and battle it

CHEER AND PREDICT
Pick the fighter you think will win and cheer them on. Guess right and you earn bonus coins!

9 BATTLE ARENAS
Grassland, Ocean, Sky, Arctic, Desert, Jungle, Volcano, Night and Storm. The arena matters: a shark on land doesn't stand a chance, and a lion can't beat a shark under the sea.

LEARN AS YOU PLAY
- "WHY?" card: the real reason the winner won
- Tale of the Tape: weight, speed and diet side by side
- Sticker Book: collect every creature and read its animal facts

MORE WAYS TO PLAY
- Tournament Mode: brackets of 4, 8 or 16 creatures and a grand champion
- Team Battles: up to 4 vs 4
- Daily Challenge, streaks, trophies and a Hall of Fame
- Voice search: say a creature's name instead of typing it
- Share your best battles as picture cards
- No internet? Battles still work offline

FOR GROWN-UPS
- Purchases and outside links sit behind a grown-up check
- We never sell data. Voice search stays on the device.
- Ads are limited, non-personalized and child-directed
- Battle stories are written by AI under strict kid-friendly rules (exciting, never gory) and checked before they're shown

IN-APP PURCHASES
Creature packs (Prehistoric, Fantasy, Mythic Beasts, Olympus), the Arena Pack, the Melee team-battle pack, coin packs, Remove Ads, and the Everything Bundle, which includes every pack plus no ads.

Premium subscription (monthly or yearly): every creature and arena, no ads, and double coins. Payment is charged to your Apple Account at confirmation of purchase. The subscription renews automatically unless it is canceled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period, and you can manage or cancel it in your Apple Account settings.

## Claims removed from the 1.1.6 listing, and why

- "80+ creatures" / "62+ real animals": there are 143.
- Anacondas, polar bears, "saltwater crocodiles": not in the game.
- "Animated health bars drain as each fighter lands hits, critical strikes": that battle screen was removed.
- "Mount Olympus" arena: the 9 arenas don't include it.
- "AI decides who wins": real size and abilities decide; the AI only tells the story.
- "Every battle teaches real facts ... loved by classrooms": fun facts are AI-written and usually, but not always, accurate; no classroom evidence.
- Price list: Mythic ($2.99) and Olympus ($19.99) were listed at $1.99; prices now come from the store page itself.
- Premium "unlimited battles": free players have the same battle limits, so it isn't a Premium perk.
- "Grand-champion wagers": reworded; betting language doesn't belong in a kids' listing.
