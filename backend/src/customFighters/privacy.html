Privacy Policy

AvA — Animal vs Animal (“Who Would Win?”) for iPhone & iPad
Published by King Painter Technology LLC · Effective 2026-04-19 · Last updated 2026-09-26

AvA is made for children and families, and we take that seriously. This policy explains — in plain language — exactly what the app does and does not do with information. “We,” “us,” and “our” mean King Painter Technology LLC, the company that makes and operates AvA and the animal-vs-animal.com services.

Plain-English Summary

  • The base game needs no account. The optional 2.0 custom-artwork library uses Sign in with Apple; we do not request your name, email, or phone number.
  • We don’t track you or your child across other apps or websites, and we don’t sell any data.
  • Ads are kid-safe: non-personalized, configured for child-directed treatment, rated G — and a one-time purchase removes them.
  • Purchases are handled entirely by Apple. We never see card numbers or billing details.
  • Game progress lives on your device and optionally in your own iCloud. If you use the optional custom-artwork library, its fighter names and artwork are also saved privately on our server.
  • If you use a custom creature, its filtered name is sent to our battle service to write the story and, when needed, find a picture.
  • A random app-install identifier and Apple App Attest security proof protect the paid API from bots; neither is used for advertising or linked to an account.
  • Voice search happens on the device. Audio never leaves the phone.
  • Grown-ups can erase device/iCloud game progress and downloaded artwork in the Grown-Up Zone. To delete the online custom-artwork library, use My Fighters → Account → Delete fighter library.

1.Information the app does not collect

To be explicit, the app does not collect any of the following:

  • A requested real name, email address, phone number, or postal address
  • Age or birthdate
  • Your Apple password (optional library sign-in is handled by Apple)
  • Precise location (GPS)
  • Photos, videos, files, or recorded audio from your device
  • Contacts, calendar, messages, or call history
  • Browsing history or activity in other apps
  • Health, fitness, financial, or biometric data

We do not add a general-purpose analytics or crash-reporting SDK such as Google Analytics, Firebase Analytics, Crashlytics, or Sentry. The AdMob advertising SDK has the limited ad-delivery diagnostics described below.

2.Information the app does handle

2.1 Saved on your device

The app stores game data on your device so progress persists between sessions: sound/narration/haptics preferences, battle counts, win streaks and per-animal win records, coin balance, sticker and achievement unlocks, purchased packs, and any custom creature names you’ve created.

If iCloud is enabled on the device, key progress syncs through your own iCloud account (Apple’s key-value storage) so it survives getting a new phone. That data lives in your Apple account — we never see it.

2.2 Sent to our battle service

When you start a battle (or a team battle, with up to six fighters per side), the app sends the fighters’ names (or the custom names you typed), the chosen arena (only if one was chosen), and — for tournaments — a standardized round label to our battle service at api.animal-vs-animal.com, which we operate. The service uses Anthropic’s Claude model to write cloud battle stories, so those creature names are processed by Anthropic to generate the text.

Custom creature names pass through content filters before use. Our service does not retain raw custom names or name-derived identifiers in battle statistics. A generated response can remain in a protected, hashed-key cache for up to 24 hours so retries do not create duplicate AI charges, after which it is deleted.

Every installation creates a random identifier, and supported Apple devices use App Attest, to enforce quotas and confirm that paid requests come from a genuine copy of the app. We also process a one-way keyed hash derived from the network address for short-term abuse prevention. Rate-limit records expire after about one hour; unused App Attest public-key records are deleted after 180 days. These security identifiers are not connected to an account, used for advertising, or shared with other users.

2.3 Custom creature pictures

Version 2.0 uses bundled retro artwork for ordinary custom fighters. In earlier versions, after you choose a custom creature, the app may look for a picture using the filtered creature name only:

  • Wikipedia (Wikimedia Foundation) is tried first for a real photo — their privacy policy applies to that lookup.
  • If no photo exists, Pollinations.ai generates a G-rated cartoon from a fixed, child-friendly prompt containing only the filtered creature name.

Only the creature name is sent — never anything about you or your device.

2.4 Ads (Google AdMob)

The free version shows ads through Google AdMob with these settings locked in:

  • Child age-restricted treatment: ON
  • Maximum ad content rating: G
  • Personalized ads and publisher first-party identifier: OFF (contextual only)

To show and count ads, Google’s ad software receives standard device information (device identifiers, an approximate location derived from IP address, and how ads perform in the app). With the child-directed settings above, Google does not use behavioral profiling or cross-app tracking for these ads — see Google’s child-directed ads documentation and Google’s privacy policy. We do not use Apple’s App Tracking Transparency because we do not track. Removing ads (one-time purchase, Premium, or the Everything Bundle) stops all interstitial ads and their ad requests. One optional feature remains for everyone — the “watch a video to earn coins” button in the Coin Shop — and the ad network is only contacted for it when you open the Coin Shop.

2.5 Diagnostics

AvA does not send Apple MetricKit crash or hang reports to our service. When the AdMob SDK is active, Google says it may receive non-user-related crash logs and performance information to operate, secure, and improve ad delivery; these items are disclosed in the App Store privacy label and are handled under Google’s privacy policy.

2.6 Purchases

All payments run through Apple’s StoreKit. We receive only a receipt confirming a purchase succeeded — never your card number, billing address, or identity. Real-money purchases in the app sit behind a parental gate, and Apple’s Ask to Buy is fully supported.

2.7 Voice search & read-aloud

Voice search uses the device microphone with Apple’s speech recognition locked to on-device only — audio and transcripts are processed on the phone and are never sent anywhere. On a device that can’t do on-device recognition, voice search simply turns itself off (you can type instead) — it never falls back to server processing. Read-aloud narration uses the device’s built-in text-to-speech. Both require your permission via iOS settings and are entirely optional.

2.8 Game Center (optional)

If you choose to sign in to Apple’s Game Center, achievements are saved under your Apple account, handled by Apple per Apple’s privacy policy. The app works fully without it.

2.9 Custom artwork and My Fighters (optional 2.0 beta)

A grown-up can connect a private fighter library using Sign in with Apple. We verify Apple's signed identity information, derive a private library identifier, and store a protected session on the device. We do not request a name or email and do not keep identity tokens in application logs. Subscription receipts are verified and associated with this library to enforce the artwork allowance; we do not receive payment-card information.

When a grown-up reviews the artwork disclosure and chooses Create, the entered fighter idea is sent to OpenAI for content checking, image generation, and artwork quality checking. Only approved four-pose artwork is delivered. OpenAI processes these requests under its applicable API data policies. Do not enter contact details or other private information. Saved artwork can be reused without another image-generation request.

Our Railway-hosted private library stores the fighter name, accepted original sprite sheet, four-pose artwork, creation details, and safety/quality status. These assets remain available when a subscription expires, until you delete them. We do not automatically publish private fighters, make entered names searchable by other players, or add them to the shared game catalog. Wider reuse would require a separate permission and review process.

You can report artwork from My Fighters. An unsafe-art report hides the artwork from the server library and removes the reporting device's downloaded copy. Other connected devices apply removal when they next refresh. A device that is offline cannot receive a new removal instruction until it reconnects.

Deleting the library invalidates its app sessions. In this internal beta, it does not revoke the underlying Sign in with Apple authorization; a grown-up can remove that authorization in their Apple account settings. Historical hosting backups may retain deleted records until those backups expire; live-service deletion does not immediately erase past backups.

3.Children’s privacy

AvA is designed for children and families. Its privacy protections include:

  • The base game needs no account or contact information. A grown-up controls optional private-library sign-in and generated-artwork requests; obvious contact details are rejected from custom-creature input.
  • No chat, social features, user-to-user messaging, or public sharing of typed content.
  • Everything a child types is checked by a content filter before it can be used.
  • Ads are contextual only — no behavioral profiling, no retargeting, ever.
  • A parental gate protects purchases, external links, private-library sign-in, and new artwork creation. The creation screen explains third-party processing before a request is sent.

If you believe your child’s information was somehow collected in error, email billvoyiatzis@gmail.com and we will delete it immediately.

4.Data retention & deletion

  • On your device: game progress, the grown-up PIN, downloaded artwork and local library sign-in can be removed in the Grown-Up Zone. Erasing game data also clears the app's iCloud-synced progress. This does not delete the private online fighter library or cancel a subscription.
  • Private artwork library: delete a fighter in My Fighters, or delete the entire online library in My Fighters → Account. Library deletion cancels pending jobs and removes private names and artwork from the live service. Minimal usage, transaction and duplicate-request records remain where needed for billing, abuse prevention and preventing a deleted request from being charged again. Operational usage details are pruned after 90 days and unsuccessful-request names after seven days; approved-art provenance remains with the owned asset until deletion.
  • Our service records: raw custom names are not retained in battle statistics; transient generated-result caches expire within 24 hours, abuse counters within about one hour, and inactive App Attest keys after 180 days.
  • Wikipedia / Pollinations requests: handled by those services under their own policies.
  • Purchases & Game Center: retained by Apple under Apple’s terms.

For any other deletion request, email billvoyiatzis@gmail.com. To authenticate ownership of a private fighter library, use the in-app library account controls.

5.Who receives any data

We never sell or rent information, and we never share it for advertising beyond what’s described above. The only parties that process any data are:

  • Apple — StoreKit purchases, optional Game Center, optional iCloud sync, and optional private-library authentication
  • Google — AdMob ads with child-directed settings
  • Anthropic — creature names and arena labels are processed by Claude when cloud narration or custom-creature classification is requested
  • OpenAI — the entered idea and generated artwork when you explicitly request custom artwork in the 2.0 beta
  • Wikimedia Foundation & Pollinations.ai — custom-creature picture lookups
  • Our hosting provider (Railway) — runs our battle service and optional private custom-artwork library

6.Security

All network traffic to our services and partners uses HTTPS, enforced by Apple’s App Transport Security. Apple App Attest, request signing, strict quotas, duplicate-request protection, and application spending ceilings protect the battle service.

7.International users

The app is available worldwide. If you are in the EEA, UK, California, or another region with data-protection laws, email us with any access or deletion request and we’ll help. Base-game security identifiers are pseudonymous, so we may be unable to associate them with an installation. The optional private fighter library has authenticated in-app deletion controls.

8.Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time; the “Last updated” date above shows the current version. Material changes are announced in the app’s release notes.
